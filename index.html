Lectern

A self-hosted email desk for a university professor.

Lectern runs on its owner's own computer or server. It reads the owner's inbox (Gmail, Zoho Mail or Microsoft 365), shows each email with a short AI summary, and writes a draft reply the owner can edit. A reply is sent only when the owner presses Send, and only to the people on the original email.

  • Sorts the day's mail by what needs attention: replies due today, this week, and newsletters.
  • Finds deadlines mentioned in email.
  • Drafts replies in the owner's voice, leaving anything it can't know as a blank to fill in.

Who can use it

Lectern is not a hosted service and has no public sign-up. Each copy is installed and used by one person, who connects their own mail accounts. Nobody else, including the author, can see the mail it handles.
