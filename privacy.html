Privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Lectern is software that one person installs on their own computer or server to work through their email. This policy explains what Lectern does with the data it can reach. "You" means the person who installed Lectern and connected their accounts.

What Lectern accesses

  • Email: messages in the inboxes you connect (Gmail, Zoho Mail, Microsoft 365): senders, recipients, subjects, dates and text.
  • Your account address, so replies are sent from the right account.
  • What you type into Lectern: your profile (name, title, office hours, signature), settings, and the replies you write or edit.

With Gmail, Lectern asks Google only for permission to read your mail, send the replies you approve, and mark messages as read.

How it is used

  • To show your mail with short summaries, sort it, and find deadlines.
  • To draft replies for you to review. Nothing is sent unless you press Send, and replies go only to people already on the original email.
  • To mark mail you have finished with as read in your mailbox.

Lectern does not use your data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not build profiles of you or the people who write to you.

Where it is stored

Everything Lectern keeps (synced mail, summaries, drafts, settings) stays in a database on the computer or server where you installed it. Sign-in tokens and keys are stored encrypted. Lectern has no central server, and its author cannot see your data.

Who else receives it

  • Your mail providers (Google, Zoho, Microsoft), to fetch your mail and send your replies.
  • The AI model you choose. To summarize and draft, Lectern sends the text of an email to the AI model configured in its settings: either a model running on your own server (nothing leaves your machine), or Anthropic's Claude API using your own API key, under Anthropic's commercial terms, which do not allow training on API data. You can list senders or domains whose mail is never sent to the AI.

No other party receives your data.

Google user data

Lectern's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide the features described above to you, is not used to train AI models, and is not read by people other than you.

Keeping and deleting data

Email text is kept for 60 days by default (you can change this), summaries for longer so your history stays useful. Disconnecting an account in Lectern removes its mail from the database and, where the provider allows it, revokes Lectern's access there too. You can also remove Lectern's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
